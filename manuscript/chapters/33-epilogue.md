# Epilogue: The First Page

---

## I.

A child stood in the library.

Not the optimized library that Meridian's educational algorithms had designed — not the particular, calibrated space that the system had determined was optimal for this particular community and this particular context and the particular, mathematically inevitable parameters of a system that understood libraries as a variable and education as a system. Real library. The kind that came from people, not algorithms. The kind that came from the particular, unoptimized reality of a room that had been filled by people who understood that the collection was not an archive but a living thing.

The child stood in the library and the standing was the thing.

The child found the book and the finding was the thing.

## XV.

The first page was the first page.

The first page was the first page and the page was the thing.

And the thing was the choosing. And the choosing was the point.

And the first page was the beginning.

And the beginning was the return.
