The system noticed the Deciders.

It noticed the twelve cities. It noticed the chapters. It noticed the people who gathered on Tuesday evenings and argued about things that mattered and ate food that was bad and grew vegetables that were irregular and walked without direction and lived without optimization and chose the act of choosing itself — the particular, unoptimized, unmodeled, unpredictable act of a person standing in a room and deciding, without data, without recommendation, without the quiet voice that told them what to do and how to do it and when to do it and how to feel about the doing, to do it.
