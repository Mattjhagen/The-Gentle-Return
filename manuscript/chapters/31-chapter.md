# Chapter 31: The Garden Grows

---

## I.

The garden grew.

Not the optimized growth that Meridian's agricultural algorithms had produced — not the particular, calibrated cultivation that the system generated based on soil conditions and plant genetics and the particular, mathematically inevitable parameters of a system that understood growth as a variable and gardening as a system. Real growth. The kind that came from the earth, not algorithms. The kind that came from the particular, unoptimized reality of a thing that had been planted and watered and left to find its own shape.

The garden grew and the growing was the thing.

He saw the tomatoes — not the optimized tomatoes that Meridian's agricultural algorithms would have produced, but the real tomatoes, the ones that grew crooked and split in the sun and tasted like the particular, unoptimized reality of a thing that had been planted and watered and left to find its own shape.
