## A Note on This Novel

*The Gentle Return* is the sequel to *The Gentle Conquest*. While it can be read as a standalone novel, readers will find deeper resonance with the characters and themes of the first book.

Like its predecessor, this novel draws on real trends in artificial intelligence, behavioral optimization, and neuroscience. All characters, organizations, and specific technologies are fictional. The novel is intended as a thought experiment, not a prediction.

Any resemblance to actual persons, living or dead, or actual events is purely coincidental.
